------------------------------------------------------------
 7. LIMITATIONS TO STATE CLEARLY
------------------------------------------------------------

- HDFS is a single-node development cluster with replication 1.
- Spark is local[6], not a multi-node cluster.
- Global ST-DBSCAN uses a documented 119,765-event sample because of
  the 16 GB RAM limit.
- ARI 0.999991 is near-equivalence, not bit-identical labels.
- A few border/core-boundary assignments differ.
- Mc and b-values depend on method, bin width and magnitude scales.
- Volcano results are associations, not predictions or causation.
- Dashboard maps use bounded display samples.
