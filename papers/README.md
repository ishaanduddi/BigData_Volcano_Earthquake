## Getting the three missing ones (2 minutes each)

**3 — SOM–DBSCAN.** Springer, paywalled. Use the college/library login:
<https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00521-022-08085-5> → "Download PDF".
If the institute has no Springer subscription, ask for it on ResearchGate from the authors
(Sharma / Vijay / Nanda, NIT Rourkela) — they respond quickly.

**7 — a-Positive.** AGU/Wiley, paywalled. Either:
- <https://agupubs.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1029/2023JB027089> → "PDF" (needs login), or
- the USGS record (may offer a free accepted manuscript in some browsers):
  <https://pubs.usgs.gov/publication/70252983>

**8 — Subsequence clustering.** FREE, just not scriptable:
<https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9288819/> → "PDF" button (top right).
It opens normally in a human browser; only automated retrieval is blocked.
